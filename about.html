My background

I graduated from Purdue University’s Mitch Daniels School of Business in May 2026 with Distinction and a 3.85/4.00 GPA. My coursework combined machine learning, statistics, database management, visualization, and decision modeling.

My undergraduate research explored CityBus ridership with Prof. Xing Wang, and financial transaction fraud and survivor survey classification with Prof. Matthew A. Lanham. These team projects culminated in presentations at Purdue’s undergraduate research conferences.

I now work as a Data Analyst at Infodat, where my work includes a data-quality and AutoML prototype, Airflow database connectivity, and mentoring seven graduate students on a capstone team building an AI Quality Control Agent.

What I want to study

I’m interested in data quality, automated machine learning, predictive analytics, and decision support. I want to build systems that help analysts make informed choices throughout an analysis, from preparing a dataset to evaluating a model against the problem it needs to solve.

My projects have made me especially interested in the gap between a promising model score and a useful decision: how evaluation design, error costs, and the data itself affect the conclusions we draw.
